## Graphic Generation Guidelines

When generating graphics for DHThailand, follow these rules:

1. **Always use green as dominant color** — primary: `#2E7D32`, accent: `#388E3C`
2. **White space is essential** — designs must feel clean and breathable
3. **Include brand tagline** when space allows: *"No. 1 Total Hygiene Service Solutions Since 2008"*
4. **Use DB Airy or similar clean sans-serif fonts**
5. **ECARF Quality Tested badge** — include on service-related communications
6. **German Technology** — mention where relevant (builds trust)
7. **Health/hygiene imagery** — families, clean homes, professional technicians
8. **Before-after visuals** — highly effective for this brand
9. **Thai-English bilingual** — primary in Thai, support in English for professionalism
